The Wu’s Experience

A modern expression of Korean barbecue.

Wu’s builds on the culinary heritage of Wu’s House, focused here on premium Korean barbecue — American Wagyu, USDA Prime beef, heritage pork and house marinades, cooked over the coals at your table.

The cuts

The cuts are the centerpiece.

In the restaurant’s own words — American Wagyu, USDA Prime beef and house marinades. A description of its sourcing, carried from its menu, not an independent grade.

01

American Wagyu

Rich marbling and a buttery texture — NY Strip, Boneless Rib and Denver Steak, portioned for the grill.

USDA Prime

Carefully selected premium beef — Ribeye, Brisket, Skirt Steak, Hanger and LA Galbi.

House Marinated

Classic Korean marinades prepared in-house — Bulgogi, Marinated Rib Finger and Spicy Pork.
